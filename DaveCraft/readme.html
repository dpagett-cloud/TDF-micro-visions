01GETTING STARTED

DaveCraft is an infinite, Minecraft-style 3D voxel sandbox that runs entirely in one HTML file in a modern browser (Chrome or Firefox). The world is procedurally generated from a seed, streams in around you as you walk, and never ends in any direction.

  • File: davecraft.html — everything lives in this one file
  • Needs: Chrome or Firefox + internet on first load (Three.js comes from a CDN)
  • No install, no build tools, no accounts
Tip: clicking anywhere on the title screen locks the mouse and starts the game. Press Esc any time to release the mouse.

☐ FIRST-DAY SURVIVAL GUIDE

  1. Punch a tree — hold left-click on a trunk until it breaks. Collect the Wood Log.
  2. Press E, put the log in the 2×2 grid → craft Planks.
  3. Craft Sticks (2 planks stacked), then a Crafting Table (4 planks in a square).
  4. Place the table, right-click it, craft a Wooden Pickaxe.
  5. Mine stone for a Stone Pickaxe, then hunt coal and iron.
  6. Before dark: craft torches and a sword. Night brings zombies and creepers.

03THE WORLD

INFINITE TERRAIN

The world is divided into chunks of 16 × 16 blocks, 96 blocks tall, generated on demand from the seed. Sea level is y=48; snow appears above roughly y=78.

Terrain streams in up to 8 chunks (128 blocks) around you and unloads behind you; fog hides the edge. Walk 1,000+ blocks in any direction and new terrain keeps generating — there are no boundaries, walls, or barriers.

The same seed always produces the same world: leave and return, and the terrain regenerates identically — plus your edits, which are remembered.

CAVES & ORES

  • Winding spaghetti tunnels + larger caverns, denser the deeper you go; some open at the surface as natural entrances.
  • Caves are never flooded — only open sky (oceans, lakes, rivers) fills with water.
  • Coal — common, veins of 3–8, most depths. Iron — rarer, veins of 2–6, richer the deeper you dig. Both often exposed on cave walls.
  • Bedrock — indestructible — forms a ragged 1–3 block floor at the bottom of the world.

07INVENTORY

  • 36 slots — 9 hotbar + 27 storage; stacks up to 64.
  • E opens / closes; the mouse is released while open.
  • Left-click — pick up / place / swap a stack.
  • Right-click — pick up half a stack, or place one item.
  • Closing returns crafting-grid contents (and cursor stack) to your inventory.
  • Drops stack into existing piles first, then empty slots; full inventory → items stay in the world.
  • Hotbar always visible; selected slot highlighted, item name flashes on switch.

08HEALTH & DAMAGE

  • 10 hearts (20 HP) shown above the hotbar.
  • Fall damage from drops over ~3 blocks — water breaks your fall.
  • Zombies damage on contact; creepers explode for up to 14 damage at point-blank and destroy terrain (blocks drop as items).
  • Health regenerates — 1 HP / 3 s after 8 s without damage.
  • Death → "You died!" screen → click to respawn at your spawn point with full health. Inventory is kept.
Watch out: the red vignette flash means damage. Hear a hiss? A creeper is counting down — run.
Pro tip: torch the area around your base — mobs only spawn where light < 7.

09MOBS

PASSIVE ANIMALS

Cows, pigs, sheep and chickens spawn on grass in plains, forest and rainforest. They wander, pause, and flee when hit. Kills drop raw meat (chickens drop feathers). All mobs are blocky, have gravity + collision, hop one-block steps, and animate their legs.

HOSTILE MOBS

MobBehaviour
Creeper Approaches; within 3 blocks starts a 1.5 s flashing fuse, then explodes — damages you and destroys terrain. Back away to reset the fuse!
Zombie Chases within 24 blocks with one-block step-up; 3 damage on touch (1 s cooldown) with knockback.

SPAWN RULES

  • Hostiles spawn where effective light < 7 — on the surface at night, or in unlit caves during the day.
  • Torch-lit areas are safe.
  • At dawn, hostiles exposed to daylight burn away; ones in darkness survive.
  • Mobs never spawn inside blocks or water; they despawn when far away or when their chunk unloads.
  • Total mobs are capped — difficulty sets the caps (Easy 4 / Normal 10 / Hard 18 hostile; 8/10/14 passive).

10LIGHTING

  • Sky light — open sky is fully lit; light spreads down and sideways into overhangs and cave mouths with falloff, so deep caves fade to near-black.
  • Block light — torches emit light level 14, losing one level per block outward.
  • A surface's brightness is the max of the two; sky light scales with time of day — the surface dims at night while torch light stays constant, and caves are dark even at noon.
  • Placing or breaking blocks (especially torches) recomputes lighting in the affected area.
Too dark? The pause menu's Gamma slider (0.6–2.2) brightens shadows globally — great for deep caves and night play.

11DAY / NIGHT

  • A full cycle is 10 minutes by default — configurable from 1 to 30 minutes in the pause menu.
  • Sunrise → noon → sunset colours → dark night with a visible moon; sun and moon travel the sky; sky and fog shift through sunset oranges.
  • Night is when hostile mobs appear on the surface; dawn clears them.

13HUD

  • Crosshair — centre of the screen; the targeted block gets a wireframe outline.
  • Hotbar — 9 slots with icons + stack counts.
  • Hearts — health above the hotbar.
  • Held item — first-person view bottom-right; bobs while walking, sways on turns, swings on mining/attacks.
  • Debug text (top-left) — FPS, chunk, XYZ, mob & drop counts.

14POLISH — PARTICLES & SOUND

  • Block-break particles coloured to match the block; mob hit flashes; explosion smoke and sparks; torch embers flickering above lit torches.
  • All sound is synthesised at runtime (Web Audio): break/place crunches, footsteps, pickup pops, hurt sounds, zombie groans, creeper hiss, animal noises.

16TECHNICAL NOTES

  • One file, no build step: HTML + CSS + vanilla JavaScript, Three.js from a CDN.
  • All textures generated at runtime as 16×16 pixel canvases in a single atlas (nearest-neighbour filtering) — including the ones on this page.
  • Voxel lighting (sky + torch flood-fill) baked into chunk meshes as vertex attributes; a shader uniform scales sky light with time of day.
  • Chunk data in typed arrays; generation & meshing budgeted per frame — no freezes while streaming.
  • Terrain is a pure function of (seed, chunkX, chunkZ); player edits are an override map reapplied after generation (the Save option persists it).
  • World seed is 1337 (constant WORLD_SEED at the top of the file) — change it for a whole new world.
