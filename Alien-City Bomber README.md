🎮 Game Concept

Core Objective

Control a flying vehicle (alien spacecraft 🛸 or urban bomber ✈️) and destroy all structures on the landscape before crashing. Complete 3 progressive levels to win!

Progressive Difficulty

Level	Structures	Speed Modifier	Challenge
1		20			1.0x			Learn mechanics
2		25			1.15x			Faster respawns, tighter timing
3		30			1.3x			Precision bombing required

Win/Lose Conditions
✅ WIN: Destroy all structures across all 3 levels
❌ LOSE: Collision with any structure OR flying below minimum altitude

Structure Damage System
Each structure requires 3 precise hits to destroy:
🟢 Hit 1: Top 1/3 destroyed (visual damage overlay)
🟡 Hit 2: Top 2/3 destroyed (cracks appear)  
🔴 Hit 3: Completely destroyed (particle explosion + points)

Scoring System

Bomb hits structure
+25
Structure fully destroyed
+150
Level completed
+1,000 bonus
Perfect Level (no misses)
+500 extra bonus

Shared Visual Effects
