The Game of Go

An ancient strategy game of territory and capture

◆ How to Play

  • Black plays first. Players alternate placing stones on intersections.
  • Stones of the same color connected orthogonally form a group.
  • When a group has no liberties (empty adjacent points), it is captured.
  • The goal is to control more territory than your opponent.
  • Two consecutive passes end the game.

◆ Beginner Tips

  • Control the corners first — they need fewer stones to secure.
  • Keep your groups connected for more liberties and safety.
  • Think about influence and shape, not just individual stones.
  • Atari = a group has only one liberty left. Attack those!
  • Passing when nothing useful remains is a valid, wise strategy.
