March of the Orcs is actually based on a ringtone I created about 10 years ago. It’s quite an obnoxious bit of music that certainly gets attention when my phone rings. I wanted similarly dark lyrics and was once again inspired by Tolkien and in particular the purpose of the orcs, there origin and the reason for their existence.  In my mind they were bred for nothing more than war, as expendable soldiers winning by shear numbers rather than any real fighting skills.  I think the song speaks for itself!
