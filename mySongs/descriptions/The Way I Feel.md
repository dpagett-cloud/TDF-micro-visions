The Way I Feel is a classic failed romance song, he is a gambler and she thought she could change him. That rarely works!  The song builds both instrumentally, vocally and emotionally and I class this version as one of my best. It’s good old story telling in a song, something I really like, something with a start, middle and end.
This version is quite different to my original recording I made in the late 80’s. My original version was very much a pop song with lots of jangly guitars but it never really conveyed the emotion of the protagonist. This version really hits home as it builds towards the end.
