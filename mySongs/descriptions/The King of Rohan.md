The King of Rohan is another cover with changed lyrics of a song from the 1983 Paul McCartney album Give my Regards to Broad Street. Suno would not allow my to cover my own version so I produced an instrumental version then wrote more Tolkien inspired lyrics about King Théoden of Rohan. The original song was ‘Not Such a Bad Boy’
