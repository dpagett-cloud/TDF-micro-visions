The Rise and Fall of the Hammer is another song inspired by an old garage jamming session in the early 1980s. It is kind of a metaphoric lesson foreboding the destruction of mankind by way of a mythical hammer “From a time before time…”
Maybe the hammer is nuclear destruction, or climate change? Either way, I don’t think I was looking to play out any kind of warning or message, I just like the lyrics as they popped out of my head!
