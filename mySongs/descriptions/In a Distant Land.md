In a Distant Land features Tolkien inspired lyrics. The song itself is a mashup of 2 instrumental tracks I recorded back in the 1980s. One was just the bassline and a beat, I had a new bass guitar and wanted to record something with it! The other track featured a new keyboard my brother had bought made by Gem, I was experimenting with the different preset sounds. Neither had any lyrics so I asked Suno to mash them together and then I had the basis for adding lyrics. I love the energy of this track, especially the outro.
