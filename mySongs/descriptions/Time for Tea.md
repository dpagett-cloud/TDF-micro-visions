I have been based in the Jewellery Quarter in Birmingham, England and I work with folks who teach watch and clock repair, Horology. This is me romanticising some of what they do! I used a melody I wrote decades ago for the base of the song (Lara's Theme) which is prominent at the start of the song. 

Styles : 

light and bright featuring a metronymic percussion that evokes the ticking of a clock
