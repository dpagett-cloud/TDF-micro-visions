Love Light has been a labour of love over many decades and I still don’t think it’s quite finished. I have produced many versions of this song over the years and this version is the nearest I have to how I originally imagined the song when I wrote it back in the 1980s. I did recently add a first verse to the lyrics as the original song only featured one verse. I also added some other words to make it a complete song. 

Styles:

Clean male vocal, harmonies, Acoustic guitar, Electric Guitar, building atmosphere, cinematic,  jazz,  saxaphone
