The Mountain Cries Lyric's is the story of a mountain! I guess I was taking inspiration from Tolkien’s Lonely Mountain, Erebor. There is no direct reference to the mountain itself but if fits in nicely with my other Tolkien inspired songs. The music was recorded in my Mum and Dad’s garage on a cassette tape, Just a bunch of chords played over and over with a friend jamming some kind of lead. Suno did a great job of interpreting this into a driving rock track.
