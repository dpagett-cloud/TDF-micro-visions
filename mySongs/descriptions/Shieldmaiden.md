Shieldmaiden is my take on a Eurovision Song Contest entry. Building to a cinematic climax it tells the story of the Tolkien character Éowyn, who goes on to kill the Witch-King of Angmar. The music came from an instrumental I created in the 80’ called ‘Japanish’ when I was experimenting a Korg 01/W fd synthesiser my brother and O had purchased between us. I asked Suno to cover the track and prompted it to give me a more Celtic version, that then inspired me to write the lyrics.

Styles:

Celtic style, traditional instruments, moody and reflective, growing to cinematic climax
