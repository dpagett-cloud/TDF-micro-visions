Its Birthday Time is a song I wrote on my acoustic guitar. I learned fingerpicking travis style so I could play ‘Julia’ from the Beatles’ White Album but wanted to write mu own song using the same technique. After I recorded myself, I played it into Suno and asked it to cover it as a simple acoustic song. It was later February and both my brother’s and my birthday were approaching so I was inspired to write the lyrics. I did send a copy of the song to my brother on his birthday and he seemed quite impressed.
