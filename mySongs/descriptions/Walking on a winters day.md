Walking on a winters day is a reflective  love song with gentle pacing about a woman, about 28 with long dark brown hair, walking on a cold winter day thinking about getting back home to be with her lover (we never see his face).  I tried to write this song from a woman’s perspective which is a big departure for me, but I didn’t think it was too difficult, however only a woman can tell me if I hit the mark. The music was inspired by a little fingerpicking piece I composed, one that I regularly play when I pick up my acoustic guitar.

Styles:

Soft folk, acoustic guitar, gentle percussion, light orchestration
