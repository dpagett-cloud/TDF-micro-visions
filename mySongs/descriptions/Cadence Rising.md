Cadence Rising is a love song, the male protagonist takes the lead in a gentle, romantic way. The premise of the song is around the man convincing the woman to stay longer with him at the end of the night and dance with him whilst assuring her that he has her, he senses that she is tired and offers his strength to her.
The was built around the open B7 chord and something I often practiced on my acoustic guitar. The demo of this song is no more than the guitar and me singing the lyrics.  Suno interpreted the prompt to generate the final version.
