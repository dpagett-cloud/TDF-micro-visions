Brave New World is a song composed by my brother back in the early 80s. I have a cassette recording of us rehearsing the song in the garage at our parent’s house, my contribution was the guitar riff, it was a great collaboration. Suno made light work of interpreting the old recording and with a little creative prompting it basically created this version in one shot. I have tried other prompts but couldn’t better it.
