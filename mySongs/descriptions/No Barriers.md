No Barriers is a cover of a Paul McCartney song form the 1983 album and film Give My Regards to Broad Street. The original song is called ‘No Values’ and I have covered the song back in the 1980’s myself and wanted to see what Suno would make of it. However, it recognised a potential copyright infringement so it would not produce a cover version. It would however let me create an instrumental cover. I then wrote some Tolkien inspired lyrics, referring to Bilbo as the ring carrier who holds the hopes of everyone in destroying the Ring of Power whilst trying to avoid every obstacle Middle Earth threw in his way.
