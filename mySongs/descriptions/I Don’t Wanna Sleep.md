I Don’t Wanna Sleep is a vampire song! My original recording featured lots of distorted guitars and a driving beat but this version calms things down by keeping the same chord and song structure, but in a more bluesy way. I imagined a vampire constantly searching for a ‘soul mate’ (has he even got a soul”), someone to share his nights with. He imagines all the things he could do with her each night.
