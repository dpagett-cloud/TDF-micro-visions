"After the break up then getting back together again, it's never the same but we have all tried"
This is a song I wrote back in the 1990's after getting engaged, and then splitting up.  We did get back together for a little while so it is autobiographical in many ways. I must also should out to Elvis Costello who had released his album 'Mighty Like a Rose' and I feel i took some inspiration from the track 'After the Fall'
I originally made a recording of this song at home, there is no chorus as such, just verses and a guitar solo and the AI cover follows that quite well. I did envisage a duet version of this song but Suno would not comply!
