Secret Agent is a song I have been working on for decades. I always felt that it was one of my most complete and mature songs involving lots of chord changes, tempo changes and style changes mid song. I imagined a spy reflecting on his life, remembering near death encounters and always returning to some sleezy bar and ordering a cliché Martini.
This version is very cinematic and quite different to the version I recorded in the 1980s in terms of production and instrumentation. But it’s close to how I imagined the song when I first wrote it. Talent and resources limited the demo I originally produced!
