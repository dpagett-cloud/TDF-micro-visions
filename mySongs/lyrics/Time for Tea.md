(Verse 1)
    In a shop on the corner of Cobblestone Lane
    Where the sunlight it dances, through leaded windowpane
    Sits a man with a loupe and a mind that's quite sane
    Watching the movements, as time drifts away!

(Verse 2)
    He’s got springs in his pocket and brass in his hair
    And he’s fixing the time for the folks everywhere
    With a thousand faces, all looking at him
    He slows down his hear beat and calm rushes in!

(Chorus)
    Oh, it’s time for tea! And a moment to be free!
    Just you and me and the ticking of the clock!
    Tick-tock, tick-tock, let the gears all spin
    When you lose your way,  just come on in!

(Verse 3)
    He’s got silver-toothed clocks hanging up by the door
    And a grandfather standing by the stairs, so tall
    But he doesn't care much for the "less" or the "more"
    He’s content with the rhythm of his foot steps on the floor!

(Verse 4)
    With a wink of his eye and a tilt of his head
    He’ll fix up your life while you’re dreaming in bed
    Into the night to the hum of a slow turning lathe 
    In the dimly lit workshop another treasure he'll save!

(Chorus)

    Just you and me and the ticking of the clock!
    Tick-tock, tick-tock, let the gears all spin

(Bridge)
    (Tempo slows slightly; dreamy, psychedelic string arrangement enters)
    But don't let the pendulum swing you astray
    Life isn't measured by the time of the day
    For the minute is fleeting, and the hour is vast...
    In the shop on the corner he's saving the past!
    (Drum fill—sudden return to upbeat tempo!)
